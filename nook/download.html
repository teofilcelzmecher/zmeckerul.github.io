Budgie for your computer

Version 1.7.0 · Windows 10 and 11, Mac (Apple silicon and Intel) and Linux (x64 and ARM)

  • ring_volumeCalls ring, always. Close the window and Budgie waits in the tray; it starts with your computer, so a call rings even when you haven’t opened it.
  • notificationsNotifications for messages, group chats and replies, with the unread count on Budgie’s icon.
  • present_to_allShare your screen or one window, and on Windows its sound too.
  • music_noteWhat you’re playing. Your friends see the song you’re listening to and the game you’re playing, found by the app itself. Turn it off any time.
  • updateAlways the newest Budgie. Its screens come from budgiesocial.com, so there’s nothing to update. No ads.

desktop_windowsWindows

For Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). It installs for you alone, with no administrator needed.

downloadDownload for Windows

Budgie’s installer isn’t signed with a certificate yet, so Windows may say Windows protected your PC. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

laptop_macMac

For Macs with Apple silicon or Intel, on macOS 12 or newer. Open the .dmg and drag Budgie into Applications.

downloadDownload for Mac

Budgie’s Mac app isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so the first time, your Mac may say it can’t check it. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and choose Open Anyway next to Budgie. (Or a .zip instead of the .dmg.)

terminalLinux

Pick the one for your Linux. Most computers are x64; ARM is for a Raspberry Pi and ARM laptops.

ARM: .deb · .rpm · Arch · AppImage · .tar.gz

On Ubuntu 24.04 and newer, choose the .deb: AppImages need an extra step there. Budgie waits in the tray on Cinnamon, KDE, Xfce and most others; on GNOME, its icon shows with the AppIndicator extension (without it, open Budgie from your apps to bring its window back).

Good to know

  • Budgie keeps running in the tray (on a Mac, the menu bar) when you close its window, so calls and messages still reach you. To quit it, right-click its icon and choose Quit Budgie, or press Ctrl+Q (⌘Q on a Mac). Starting with your computer can be turned off there too.
  • On Windows you can share your computer’s sound along with your screen. It shares everything your computer plays, the call included, so it’s best for videos and games.
  • The files come from Budgie’s releases on GitHub. What the app keeps and sends is in the Privacy Policy (section 4).
