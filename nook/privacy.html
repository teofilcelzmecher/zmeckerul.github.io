nook Terms of Use

Privacy Policy

Version 0.1.2 · Last updated 27 October 2026

The short version

  • Everything you do in Nook is stored in your own browser, on your own device. We don’t have a copy.
  • No cookies, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. We don’t sell or share your information.
  • Nook loads nothing from other companies. The only other company involved is GitHub, which hosts the site.
  • You can export or delete all of it yourself, any time, in Settings.

1. Who is responsible

Nook is made by Teofil Ionescu-Mihai, who is the “controller” of any personal data under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact us through github.com/teofilcelzmecher or through the person who gave you your invite code.

2. What’s stored, and where

Nook stores the following in your browser’s local storage, on your device only:

WhatWhy
Your profile: username, bio, colour, shape, icon or photoTo show who wrote your posts
Your posts, comments, polls, images, drafts and the nooks you createSo what you write is there next time
Votes, Sparks, poll answers, saved posts and collectionsTo remember what you did
Nooks you joined or visited, your streak, badges and notificationsFor your home feed and progress
Your settings, such as theme, colours and filtersSo Nook looks the way you set it
A scrambled form of your invite codeSo you only enter it once
Whether you’ve seen the storage noticeSo it doesn’t keep appearing

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. It stays until you delete it in Settings → Your data → Delete everything, or clear this site’s data in your browser. If you use Nook on two devices, each keeps its own separate copy.

Moderation checks (see the Terms of Use, section 7) run on your device too. Your text is never sent anywhere to be checked.

3. Cookies

Nook doesn’t use cookies. The browser storage described above is strictly necessary to provide the app you asked to use, so under EU rules (the ePrivacy Directive) it doesn’t need consent. We tell you about it anyway, and there are no optional trackers to accept or refuse.

4. Hosting by GitHub

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (USA). Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and basic browser information when it sends you the site’s files, and may keep logs for security. We do this because we have a legitimate interest in delivering the site reliably and securely (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). We don’t receive or see these logs. GitHub explains how it handles this data, including transfers outside the EU, in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Nook’s fonts, icons and colour library are served from the same site, so no request goes to Google, a content delivery network or any other company.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and move your personal data, and to object to how it’s used. Since your data lives on your own device, you can do most of this yourself:

  • See and take a copy: Settings → Your data → Export my data.
  • Correct: edit your profile, posts and comments.
  • Delete: Settings → Your data → Delete everything.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, for example in the country where you live.

If you’re in the United States: we don’t sell or share personal information, and don’t use it for targeted advertising. That’s true under the California Consumer Privacy Act and other state privacy laws.

6. Automatic decisions

Nook’s moderation rules decide automatically whether some text can be posted (see the Terms of Use, section 7). They run on your device, only look at the text you’re about to post, and don’t make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you. If you think a rule got it wrong, contact us and a person will look at it.

7. People named in the credits

Nook’s credits name the people who made and tested it, with their agreement. If you’re named and want your name changed or removed, contact us and we’ll do it.

8. Children

Nook isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them. We don’t collect anyone’s information at all at the moment. Between 13 and your country’s age of digital consent (up to 16 in the EU), you need a parent’s or guardian’s permission to use Nook.

9. When this will change

We plan to add accounts and a server so friends can see each other’s posts. That means some information will be stored on a server. Before that happens we’ll update this policy to say exactly what is stored, where, for how long and who provides the server, and tell you in the app first.
