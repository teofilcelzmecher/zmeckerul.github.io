Privacy Policy

Version 0.5.0 · Last updated 28 October 2026

The short version

  • Your account, posts, comments, messages and settings are kept on Budgie’s own server: a computer run by Budgie’s developer, not a big tech company.
  • We don’t ask for your email, real name, phone number or location, and Budgie’s server doesn’t log IP addresses.
  • Budgie shows ads from Google between posts in feeds, never in chats. They aren’t personalised, and Google uses cookies for them only as far as you allow (section 6).
  • Budgie itself uses no cookies, analytics or tracking, and we never sell your information or give Google your posts, messages or account.
  • To keep Budgie safe, what you post and send, private messages included, is checked automatically on Budgie’s own server, by word lists and two small AI models. Nothing is sent to another company for this, and a moderator only sees a private message that’s reported or flagged (section 8).
  • You can download everything we hold about you, or delete your account, any time in Settings → Account.

3. Why we’re allowed to (legal bases)

  • To provide Budgie to you (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)): your account, what you post, your friends and messages, and your settings.
  • Our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)): keeping Budgie secure and running (rate limits, backups, hosting) and keeping it safe (moderation, the automatic checks in section 8, and reports). You can object; contact us.
  • Showing ads, which keeps Budgie free: our legitimate interest in funding it with non-personalised ads (Article 6(1)(f)), and your consent wherever the law requires it for cookies or similar storage (Article 6(1)(a) and the ePrivacy rules). You can withdraw that consent any time (section 6).
  • Legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)): when the law requires us to act on illegal content or to answer the authorities.

4. Where it’s kept, and who else is involved

Budgie’s server is a single computer looked after by Budgie’s developer. The database on it is protected by the computer’s security, and nightly backups are kept on the same computer, with occasional copies on a storage drive in the developer’s care. As the person who runs the server, the developer can technically open the database, private messages included. We only do so to fix problems, handle reports about a specific message (including one the automatic checks flagged), or when the law requires it. The automatic checks (section 8) run on that same computer. The text model they use was downloaded once from Hugging Face, a website for sharing AI models; nothing about you or what you post is ever sent there.

  • Tailscale (Tailscale Inc., Canada) connects the server to the internet. Your connection is encrypted all the way to Budgie’s server, so Tailscale can’t read what you send, but it handles your IP address to route the connection. See the Tailscale Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA) hosts the website itself. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and basic browser information when it sends you the site’s files, and may keep logs for security. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • Google (Google Ireland Limited if you’re in the EU, the UK or Switzerland, Google LLC elsewhere) provides the ads, through Google AdSense. When a feed shows an ad, your browser loads it from Google, and Google receives your IP address, details about your browser and device, and the address of the page. We never give Google your posts, messages, username or anything else from your account. We ask Google for non-personalised ads, so the ads you see aren’t based on what you’ve done before, on Budgie or elsewhere. Google still uses cookies or similar storage for them, to limit how often you see the same ad, to stop fraud and to count views and clicks. Google decides how it uses that data, as an independent controller: see How Google uses information from sites that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

All three may process data outside the EU, under their own safeguards for international transfers. Budgie’s own fonts, icons and colour library come from the same site; the only thing loaded from another company is Google’s ads.

5. How long we keep it

  • Your account and what you post stay until you delete them.
  • When you delete a post or comment, its content is wiped from the server at once. When you change or remove your profile picture or banner, the old one is deleted from the server at once. When you delete your account, your profile, votes, Sparks, friends, messages (on both sides), notifications and images are deleted, and your posts and comments are wiped and marked “deleted”.
  • Backups are overwritten within 14 days, so deleted things are gone from backups after that.
  • Sign-ins you don’t use for 90 days expire. Your inbox keeps your newest 200 notifications.
  • Reports and moderation decisions are kept as a record for up to a year after they’re resolved.

6. In your browser

Budgie itself doesn’t use cookies. It keeps one thing in your browser’s storage: your sign-in key, so you stay signed in. That’s strictly necessary for the service you asked for, so under EU rules (the ePrivacy Directive) it doesn’t need consent. Signing out removes it.

The ads are different: Google may keep cookies or similar data in your browser for them. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent message asks you first. If you say no, you get limited ads that don’t use that data for advertising. You can change your answer any time in Settings → Privacy and legal → Ad cookie choices. Wherever you are, you can also manage Google’s ads in My Ad Center and block cookies in your browser’s settings.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and move your personal data, and to object to how it’s used. You can do most of this yourself:

  • See and take a copy: Settings → Account → Export my data.
  • Correct: edit your profile, posts and comments.
  • Delete: Settings → Account → Delete account, or delete single posts, comments and photos.

For anything else, contact us (section 1). We’ll answer within a month. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, for example in the country where you live.

If you’re in the United States: we don’t sell your personal information, and because we ask Google for non-personalised ads, we don’t use it for targeted advertising. That’s true under the California Consumer Privacy Act and other state privacy laws.

8. Automatic checks and decisions

Budgie checks what’s posted or sent automatically, to keep it safe (see the Terms of Use, section 7): with word lists on your device and on the server, and on the server with two small AI models, one that reads text and one that looks at pictures. They run on Budgie’s own computer, and nothing is sent to another company. They only look at what’s being posted or sent at that moment, keep no record of it beyond a short reason when they report something to a moderator, and aren’t used to build a profile of you or for ads.

They decide automatically whether something can be posted, but they don’t make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you: you can put a blocked post another way, and only a person can take down something already posted or ban an account. Reports are reviewed by a person. If you think a check or a decision got it wrong, contact us and a person will look at it.

9. Security

Connections to Budgie are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored with scrypt, a slow one-way hash, and sign-in keys and recovery codes only as hashes. The server only answers Budgie’s own website and only listens behind its encrypted tunnel. If something ever goes wrong with your data, we’ll tell you and, where the law requires it, the authorities.

10. People named in the credits

Budgie’s credits name the people who made and tested it, with their agreement. If you’re named and want your name changed or removed, contact us and we’ll do it.

11. Children

Budgie isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly let them sign up. Between 13 and your country’s age of digital consent (up to 16 in the EU), you need a parent’s or guardian’s permission to use Budgie. Ads on Budgie aren’t personalised for anyone, whatever their age. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

12. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we’ll tell you in the app before it applies. The date at the top always shows the latest version.
