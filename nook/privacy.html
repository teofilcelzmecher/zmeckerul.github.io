Privacy Policy

Version 0.2.1 · Last updated 27 October 2026

The short version

  • Your account, posts, comments, messages and settings are kept on Budgie’s own server: a computer run by Budgie’s developer, not a big tech company.
  • We don’t ask for your email, real name, phone number or location, and we don’t log IP addresses.
  • No cookies, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. We don’t sell or share your information.
  • You can download everything we hold about you, or delete your account, any time in Settings → Account.

1. Who is responsible

Budgie (called Nook until October 2026) is made and run by Teofil Ionescu-Mihai, who is the “controller” of your personal data under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact us through github.com/teofilcelzmecher or through the person who gave you your invite code.

We don’t store your IP address. To slow down people guessing passwords, the server briefly counts attempts per address in its memory. That count is never saved and disappears within the hour.

Images are shown through long, random links. Anyone who has an image’s exact link can open it, so don’t upload anything you wouldn’t want shared.

3. Why we’re allowed to (legal bases)

  • To provide Budgie to you (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)): your account, what you post, your friends and messages, and your settings.
  • Our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)): keeping Budgie secure and running (rate limits, backups, hosting) and keeping it safe (moderation and reports). You can object; contact us.
  • Legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)): when the law requires us to act on illegal content or to answer the authorities.

4. Where it’s kept, and who else is involved

Budgie’s server is a single computer looked after by Budgie’s developer. The database on it is protected by the computer’s security, and nightly backups are kept on the same computer, with occasional copies on a storage drive in the developer’s care. As the person who runs the server, the developer can technically open the database, private messages included. We only do so to fix problems, handle reports about a specific message, or when the law requires it.

  • Tailscale (Tailscale Inc., Canada) connects the server to the internet. Your connection is encrypted all the way to Budgie’s server, so Tailscale can’t read what you send, but it handles your IP address to route the connection. See the Tailscale Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA) hosts the website itself. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and basic browser information when it sends you the site’s files, and may keep logs for security. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Both may process data outside the EU, under their own safeguards for international transfers. Budgie’s fonts, icons and colour library come from the same site, so no request goes to Google or any other company.

5. How long we keep it

  • Your account and what you post stay until you delete them.
  • When you delete a post or comment, its content is wiped from the server at once. When you delete your account, your profile, votes, Sparks, friends, messages (on both sides), notifications and images are deleted, and your posts and comments are wiped and marked “deleted”.
  • Backups are overwritten within 14 days, so deleted things are gone from backups after that.
  • Sign-ins you don’t use for 90 days expire. Your inbox keeps your newest 200 notifications.
  • Reports and moderation decisions are kept as a record for up to a year after they’re resolved.

6. In your browser

Budgie doesn’t use cookies. It keeps one thing in your browser’s storage: your sign-in key, so you stay signed in. That’s strictly necessary for the service you asked for, so under EU rules (the ePrivacy Directive) it doesn’t need consent. Signing out removes it.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and move your personal data, and to object to how it’s used. You can do most of this yourself:

  • See and take a copy: Settings → Account → Export my data.
  • Correct: edit your profile, posts and comments.
  • Delete: Settings → Account → Delete account, or delete single posts, comments and photos.

For anything else, contact us (section 1). We’ll answer within a month. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, for example in the country where you live.

If you’re in the United States: we don’t sell or share personal information, and don’t use it for targeted advertising. That’s true under the California Consumer Privacy Act and other state privacy laws.

8. Automatic decisions

Budgie’s moderation rules decide automatically whether some text can be posted (see the Terms of Use, section 7). They run on your device and again on the server, only look at the text being posted, and don’t make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you. Reports are reviewed by a person. If you think a rule or a decision got it wrong, contact us and a person will look at it.

9. Security

Connections to Budgie are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored with scrypt, a slow one-way hash, and sign-in keys and recovery codes only as hashes. The server only answers Budgie’s own website and only listens behind its encrypted tunnel. If something ever goes wrong with your data, we’ll tell you and, where the law requires it, the authorities.

10. People named in the credits

Budgie’s credits name the people who made and tested it, with their agreement. If you’re named and want your name changed or removed, contact us and we’ll do it.

11. Children

Budgie isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly let them sign up. Between 13 and your country’s age of digital consent (up to 16 in the EU), you need a parent’s or guardian’s permission to use Budgie. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

12. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we’ll tell you in the app before it applies. The date at the top always shows the latest version.
