Terms of Use

Version 0.6.0 · Last updated 28 October 2026

The short version

  • Budgie is new: things may change or break. It’s free, and shows ads between posts.
  • You must be at least 13. Younger than the age of digital consent where you live? Ask a parent or guardian first.
  • What you write is yours. Be kind, stay legal, and don’t post other people’s private details.
  • What you post and send is checked automatically on Budgie’s own server, by word lists and by two small AI models (one for text, one for pictures). They block clearly harmful things and tell you why, and send doubtful ones to a person. Reports are reviewed by a person.
  • Keep your password and recovery code to yourself. You can delete your account any time.

3. Who can use Budgie

You must be at least 13 years old. If the law where you live sets a higher age for agreeing to online services on your own (in the EU this is between 13 and 16, depending on the country), you need permission from a parent or guardian to use Budgie. If you’re a parent and think your child is using Budgie without permission, contact us.

4. Your account

  • One account per person. Choose a username that isn’t offensive and doesn’t pretend to be someone else.
  • When you create an account, Cloudflare Turnstile may check you’re not a robot. It usually passes by itself; sometimes it asks you to tick a box. Don’t make accounts with bots or scripts.
  • Keep your password and recovery code secret. You’re responsible for what happens on your account. If you think someone else has got in, change your password in Settings → Account and tell us.
  • Your profile (username and its font, picture, banner, colour, shape and bio) can be seen by everyone on Budgie. Your picture, banner and bio follow the same rules as posts (section 6): nothing sexual, violent or hateful, and no photo of someone else without their permission. Pictures and banners are checked automatically when you upload them: if the check isn’t sure one is OK for everyone, it’s refused and you can pick another. A moderator can clear a picture, banner or bio that breaks the rules, and you’ll be told why.
  • You can delete your account any time in Settings → Account. See the Privacy Policy for what that removes.

4a. Friends and messages

You can send friend requests, and friends can message each other privately. The same rules apply in messages as everywhere else (section 6), and messages are checked automatically before they’re sent (section 7). A moderator only sees a message that’s reported, or that the automatic check flags as a possible warning sign of grooming or as sexual. Don’t keep messaging someone who has removed you or declined your request.

5. Your content

You keep ownership of everything you post: posts, comments, images, polls and nests you create. You give us permission to store, show and format it inside Budgie, only as far as we need to run Budgie. That permission ends when you delete the content, except where we must keep something by law.

You’re responsible for what you post. Only post things you have the right to share, and don’t upload anyone else’s photo of a person without their permission.

6. What’s not allowed

Don’t post or do anything that:

  • is illegal where you live, or helps someone else break the law;
  • harasses, bullies, threatens or encourages anyone to hurt themselves or others;
  • attacks people for their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation or age;
  • is sexual or sexually suggestive. Anything sexual involving a minor is reported to the authorities;
  • shows graphic violence or cruelty;
  • shares someone’s private information, such as their address, phone number, email, ID or passwords, without their permission;
  • is spam, a scam, phishing or malware, or tricks people into clicking something;
  • pretends to be another person or organisation;
  • copies someone else’s work without permission (copyright or trademark infringement);
  • tries to break, overload or get around Budgie’s security;
  • clicks ads on purpose to make money for anyone, or asks others to.

Each nest can also have its own rules, shown on its page. Follow them when you post there.

7. How moderation works

Budgie checks what you write before it’s posted or sent: first on your own device, with word lists, then again on Budgie’s server, with the same word lists and a small AI model that reads text. Pictures, and frames from GIFs, are checked on the server by another small AI model. Both models run on Budgie’s own computer: nothing you write or upload is sent to another company to be checked.

  • The word lists look for slurs, threats and phrases that tell people to hurt themselves, sexual requests, links to adult sites and scams (these are blocked); strong language (allowed, but hidden behind a tap for people who have the language filter on, which is the default); things that look like personal information, such as phone numbers, email addresses or card numbers (you’re warned and asked to confirm in posts and comments; in a bio or a nest’s description they aren’t allowed); and signs of spam: lots of links or mentions, shouting in capitals, long runs of repeated characters, posting the same thing twice, or posting very fast (blocked or slowed down). Each list is checked as written and with common disguises like swapped letters, symbols or spacing.
  • The text model reads posts, comments, bios, nests and messages for insults, threats, attacks on people for who they are, and sexual talk, in many languages. When it’s very sure, what you wrote isn’t posted and you’re told why. When it’s less sure, it’s posted and a moderator takes a look. In private messages it only stops what it’s very sure of, and only shows a moderator a message it thinks may be sexual.
  • The picture model looks for nudity and sexual content. A picture it’s sure about isn’t posted. Profile pictures and banners are stricter, because everyone sees them: if it isn’t sure one is OK, it’s refused and you can pick another. A doubtful picture in a post is posted, and a moderator takes a look.
  • In private messages, the warning signs of grooming, such as asking someone to keep secrets from their parents or whether they’re home alone, aren’t blocked, but a moderator is shown that message.

Automatic checks get things wrong both ways: they can stop something harmless and miss something harmful. When something is blocked, Budgie tells you which rule it broke. If that was a mistake, put it another way, or tell us and we’ll improve the check. The automatic checks never take down what’s already posted or ban anyone: only a moderator can.

You can report any post, comment or person from its ⋯ menu or profile. Reports go to Budgie’s moderators, who are people, not software. If a moderator removes something of yours, you’re told what was removed and why, and you can ask us to look at it again (section 1). Serious or repeated breaks of these terms can get an account banned. A banned account can’t sign in, and its owner is told why.

8. Reporting illegal content

If you think something on Budgie is illegal, or uses your work, photo or trademark without permission, tell us using the contact details in section 1. Please include:

  • where it is (a link or a screenshot);
  • why you think it’s illegal, or which of your rights it breaks;
  • your name and email address (you can leave these out if you’re reporting child sexual abuse material);
  • a statement that your report is accurate and made in good faith.

We’ll look at it promptly. We’ll tell you what we decided, and we’ll tell the person who posted it why. If something suggests that someone’s life or safety is at risk, we may tell the police or other authorities.

8a. Ads

Budgie is free, and pays for itself with ads between posts in feeds. They come from Google AdSense: we don’t choose individual ads, and an ad isn’t a recommendation from us. If you see an ad that’s offensive, misleading or illegal, tell us (section 1) and we’ll ask Google to block it. How ads use your data is explained in the Privacy Policy.

9. Ending things

You can stop using Budgie whenever you like, and delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete account. If you seriously or repeatedly break these terms, we can ban your account. We’ll tell you why, and you can ask us to reconsider.

Nothing here takes away rights you have as a consumer under the law of the country you live in.
